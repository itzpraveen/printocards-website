The life of a card

One real-size PVC card is the main character. As the visitor scrolls, it goes through production: blank sheet, full-colour print, finish, chip inlay, and out into offices, hospitals and hotels. By the time they reach the product range, they understand what Printo makes and why it's made well. Every chapter ends one tap away from a WhatsApp quote.

Read this board top to bottom. Frames are the desktop home page at 1200 × 750, in scroll order. Blue chips and dashed boxes are annotations for motion and interaction; they are not part of the design.

Content comes from your Website.zip (26 products, specs, finishes), the current printocards.com pages, and your logo colours. Nothing is invented except sample names printed on the demo cards.

Principles

  • The card is the hero, everywhere. One WebGL card, one memorable thing. The rest of the page stays quiet so it lands.
  • Print vocabulary is structure, not decoration. Crop marks show real dimensions. The registration mark shows story progress. Chapter numbers exist because production really is a sequence.
  • Colour changes with the story. Orange stage, PVC white, lime finishes, black for the chip x-ray, then back to orange to ask for the order.
  • Every word stays in HTML. WebGL is decoration layered on real text, so Google and screen readers get everything.
  • Business first. WhatsApp and call are one tap away on every screen. No fake checkout, no invented prices.

  • Static HTML for every page, pre-rendered, so all copy is crawlable. WebGL never holds the content.
  • Structured data: LocalBusiness with both addresses, Product for each item, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
  • Page titles aimed at real searches, e.g. "PVC ID Card Printing in Calicut", "Hotel Key Cards in Kerala", "NFC Cards, NTAG 213/215/216".
  • Speed: Three.js loads after the first paint, with a still-image placeholder. Targets: LCP under 2.5 s on 4G, CLS near 0.
  • sitemap.xml, canonical URLs, Open Graph images per page, redirects from the old /about, /services and /contact.

Stack

  • Astro generates plain, fast HTML for every page and ships JavaScript only where there is motion. It's a better SEO fit than the current single-page React build.
  • Three.js for the card scenes, with custom GLSL for the print sweep and x-ray.
  • GSAP with ScrollTrigger and SplitText, all free since 2025, for scroll scenes and type.
  • Lenis for smooth scrolling.
  • Archivo variable font, self-hosted, one file.
  • Reuses your current product data, all 26 optimised images, the logos and the catalogue PDF.
  • Deploys as static files to any host, including Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or your current server.
