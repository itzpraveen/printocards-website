Questions buyers ask first.

Will your RFID cards work with our door locks or readers?

Send us the brand and model of your lock or reader. We will confirm the right chip, whether 125 kHz proximity, 13.56 MHz MIFARE or NFC, before you order.

What size are your cards?

Our PVC cards are 86 × 54 mm (CR80), the same size as a bank card. Blank cards for card printers are 30 mil, or 0.76 mm, thick.

Can each card carry its own name and photo?

Yes. ID cards are personalised to your requirements, with names, photos and ID numbers printed in full colour on one or both sides.

Which finishes can I choose?

Glossy, matte, frosted and UV, depending on the card type. Pre-printed cards come in glossy or matte.

Do you sell card printers and supplies?

Yes. We supply Evolis Primacy 2, Evolis Agilia, Evolis Zenius, Zebra ZC 300 and IDP Solid 510D printers, plus Zebra and Pacto ID blank cards and printer ribbons.

Where are you based?

Our office is at Darussalam Complex on Mavoor Road, Calicut, and our factory is at Omanoor, Kerala.
